Reflection on why websites use robots.txt:
Websites use robots.txt to communicate crawling policies to automated bots, protecting server resources and sensitive content while allowing legitimate indexing. This promotes ethical scraping by providing clear guidelines that responsible scrapers should follow to avoid overloading servers or accessing restricted areas. Respecting robots.txt ensures that automated access does not degrade the experience for human users or violate the site's terms of service.
